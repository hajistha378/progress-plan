Our little corner

Coffee, cake and a little bit of calm.

Coco Lane began with a simple idea: make a place we would want to spend our own Sunday in. Warm light, good records, properly brewed coffee and a counter full of things baked that morning.

  • Beans roasted in small batches and rested for the perfect pour.
  • Cakes and pastries made in-house, never too sweet.
  • Big windows, soft playlists and space to stay a while.

The Coco Lane way

The details are the whole point.

No shortcuts, no fuss. Just the small things that make a coffee stop feel like a proper pause.

01

Thoughtful sourcing

Single-origin beans and Ceylon tea, chosen for flavour and grown with care. We can tell you where every cup comes from.

02

Baked in-house

Brownies, cakes and croissants made in our little kitchen each morning. If it is not fresh, it does not make the counter.

03

A place to linger

Comfy chairs, big windows and no rush. Bring a book, bring a friend, or bring neither and just enjoy the quiet.
